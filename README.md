CA StudyOS
A free study planner for CA Foundation, Intermediate and Final students in India. Built by a CA aspirant, for CA aspirants.
Live app: https://kyahoagarchannel-code.github.io/studyOS/
What it does
One journey, one profile. Foundation (or direct entry) to Intermediate to Final, with the marks of every earlier attempt.
Daily plan that adapts. Uses your exam date, daily hours, past marks and mock scores. Shows just the 3 things that matter today.
Honest progress. A chapter can be ticked only after studying it with the focus timer.
Spaced revision, mistake notebook with retests, flash cards.
Mock analysis: where did the marks go (concept, calculation, silly, time, forgot).
Timed paper practice with a time budget for every question.
Past paper weightage: add the marks each chapter carried; important chapters come first.
Exam mode and checklist in the last 30 days.
Partner and groups: shared goals, a goal for tomorrow, nudges, study group leaderboard. You choose what is shared; everything is OFF by default.
Push reminders, English / Hinglish / Hindi, works offline, installable as an app, one-tap backup.
AI tutor for doubts (needs your own free Gemini key).
Privacy
Study data stays on your device unless you sign in. Sharing with a partner or a group shows only the items you switch on. Nothing is sold or shown to anyone else.
Not affiliated with ICAI
Chapter lists and exam dates are compiled from public sources and can be wrong. Always confirm on icai.org.
Tech
A single `index.html` (no build step), a service worker, and a Supabase backend (Auth, Postgres with row level security, Edge Functions). Hosted on GitHub Pages.
